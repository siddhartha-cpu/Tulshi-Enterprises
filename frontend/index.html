One accountable team for the civil work and the wiring

Headquartered at CDA, Sector-10, Cuttack, M/S. Tulshi Enterprises carries out government, railway and industrial contracts for street lighting, high masts, power lines and substations across Odisha and West Bengal.

Most delays on lighting jobs happen where the civil contractor hands over to the electrical one. We do both. The same crew excavates, casts the foundations, erects the poles, lays the cable and commissions the lights, so one team answers for the whole job.
